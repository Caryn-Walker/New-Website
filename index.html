Oven, one team, no shortcuts

A small oven, run by three people

We start each loaf the night before, let it rise slow, and shape it by hand in the morning. Nothing here is made to sit around — most of what's on the counter by 9am is gone by noon, and we're fine with that.

Ana runs the ovens, Tomás handles pastry, and Priya takes care of everything else, from cake orders to the coffee machine that's older than the shop itself.

What we make

Sourdough & daily loaves

A rotating rye, a country white, and whatever else rose well that night. Out by 7:30am, usually gone by 11.

Morning pastry

Croissants, cardamom buns, and a seasonal fruit danish. Best eaten within the hour, still warm.

Cakes to order

Birthdays, weddings, small gatherings. Tell us the size and the flavor — give us three days' notice.

Coffee

A short, honest menu: drip, espresso, and one seasonal drink that changes every couple of months.
